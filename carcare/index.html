What we are building

A connected customer-care and reputation system.

ArkHimar CarCare begins when staff mark a vehicle job complete. The system creates the Job ID, recognizes returning customers through a stable Customer ID and emails a prefilled feedback link.

Customer feedback then enters an AI-assisted review workflow. Positive responses move to a public-ready queue. Complaints receive a private draft response, while severe or repeat complaints receive management priority. Human staff remain responsible for every message sent.
