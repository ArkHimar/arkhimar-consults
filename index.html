About Arkhimar Consult

A practice built on technical rigour and design clarity.

Arkhimar Consult is an architectural and built-environment consultancy providing design, documentation and advisory services across residential, commercial and institutional projects.

We work closely with clients, understanding site constraints, statutory requirements, budget realities and construction practice — so every drawing we issue can be built with confidence.

Every engagement begins with a clear brief and ends with documentation your contractor can rely on.

How It Works

Four clear steps from enquiry to commencement

A considered process that gives every project the right foundation before design work begins.

  1. Step 01

    Tell Us About Your Project

    Complete the project request form with your requirements, location, size and budget expectations.

  2. Step 02

    We Review Your Brief

    The Arkhimar team studies your requirements, assesses feasibility and prepares initial observations.

  3. Step 03

    Consultation & Proposal

    We contact you to clarify the brief and present the appropriate professional engagement and proposal.

  4. Step 04

    Project Commencement

    Once terms are agreed, the architectural design process begins with a clear programme of work.
