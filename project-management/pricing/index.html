PRICING FAQ

Clear by design.

When does billing start?

Free requires no card. Growth and Professional billing starts only after you review and authorize the payment on the secure checkout page.

Who counts as a paid seat?

An internal team member who actively manages project data. Sponsor, client and reviewer guest policies are being validated separately.

Is AI unlimited?

No. ArkHimar AI Copilot is planned with useful included allowances and transparent additional usage. It is not yet live.

How does annual billing work?

Growth is $8 and Professional is $16 per user per month when billed annually. The full annual amount is displayed and authorized at checkout.
