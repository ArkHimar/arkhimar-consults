PRICING FAQ

Clear by design.

Does joining early access start billing?

No. The current programme is a private product beta; there is no live checkout.

Who counts as a paid seat?

An internal team member who actively manages project data. Sponsor, client and reviewer guest policies are being validated separately.

Is AI unlimited?

No. ArkHimar AI Copilot is planned with useful included allowances and transparent additional usage. It is not yet live.

Are annual prices approved?

They are the current commercial direction: Growth at $8 and Professional at $16 per user/month billed annually, subject to owner validation before checkout launches.
