PRIVATE-BETA NOTICE · UPDATED 6 SEPTEMBER 2026

Privacy in plain language.

This summary describes the current public marketing and browser-validation experience. A reviewed production privacy policy will be required before account-based launch.

Marketing and early-access forms

We collect the fields you choose to submit so ArkHimar Consult can respond to your enquiry. Forms are delivered to projects@arkhimar.com through FormSubmit and the configured email route. Do not submit confidential project information.

Validation workspace data

The current `/pm/` validation workspace stores project records in local browser storage on your device. It is not a secure multi-user cloud workspace. Clearing site data may remove those records.

Analytics

The site includes a provider-neutral event interface. It does not send project narratives, financial values, risk descriptions, stakeholder identities or document contents to generic analytics. No third-party analytics provider is currently configured in this repository.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit a form. To request correction or deletion of information you submitted, contact projects@arkhimar.com with enough detail to identify the request.

Production changes

Authentication, cloud persistence, billing and new service providers will require this notice to be updated before those capabilities launch.
